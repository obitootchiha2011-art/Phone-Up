The Galaxy Fold X represents Samsung's most refined foldable to date. The crease on the inner display is nearly imperceptible during everyday use, and the hinge mechanism feels noticeably more solid than previous generations.

Performance is a clear highlight — the Snapdragon 8 Elite variant handles multitasking across the 7.6-inch inner display without any stutter, making this one of the few foldables that genuinely replaces a tablet for light productivity work.

The camera system punches above what we'd expect from a foldable form factor, though it still trails dedicated camera flagships in low-light telephoto shots. Battery life comfortably covers a full day of moderate use, but heavy users relying on the inner display will want to charge by early evening.

Overall, this is the most complete foldable Samsung has released, held back mainly by its price and the inherent compromises of the category rather than any specific flaw in execution.
